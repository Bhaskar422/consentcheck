How one audit runs

01 · OPENDisposable sandboxChromium in a fresh gVisor container, network only through the egress guard
02 · PLANqwen3.8-flash-nextreads the screenshot and buttons, picks the control that refuses consent
03 · POLICY GATEDeterministic checkonly reject / settings / save clicks; Accept, paywall and partial opt-outs are refused
04 · VERIFYminimax-m3.1-flasha different model family compares before/after screenshots
05 · WATCHTwo witnessesbrowser request log + egress-guard proxy log, then a reload as a returning visitor
06 · VERDICTFrom evidence, not AItracker catalogue, cookies, Google consent-mode signals → FAIL / REVIEW / PASS
07 · REPORTmimo-v2.6-flashplain-English summary, legal context and fixes from the computed findings
08 · HUMANReviewer approvesSHA-256 manifest of every artifact, printable A4 evidence report

Slow, wrong or hostile model? Replies are schema-checked, the gate overrules unsafe picks, and heuristics take over.
